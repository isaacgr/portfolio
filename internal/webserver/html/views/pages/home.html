About me

Career

Since 2016 I've worked at Evertz Microsystems, a broadcast equipment manufacturer based in Canada. The broadcast industry can be best described as 'unique' and I think my experience in this field offers some different insights and viewpoints on software.

My work is with the control system we offer, MAGNUM, focusing heavily on the design and SDVN architecture side of our projects. I am deeply involved in system deployments, designs, diagnostics and short-to-long term support strategies.

Personal

While my educational discipline was in electrical engineering, I found myself in a software role with work, which forced me to integrate with this more over time. My personal projects list isnt particularily extensive but I try to be consistent. I probably also spend too much time on work projects after hours, which I can't share.

I do still try to work with hardware when I can, so I combine my interests ocassionally by building projects with IOT devices, or some purely discrete components. I'm also in the process of learning to play the bass.
